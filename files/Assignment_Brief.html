In This Town: film your own Roblox story

Digital Culture, grade 6 and up. Five lessons. Deadline: the last day of the month.

What is this about?

You've probably seen "In this town…" videos: Roblox characters, a strange rule on screen ("In this town, if nobody finds you in hide and seek for an hour, you turn invisible forever"), then someone breaks it, and at the end comes the twist.

This month you make one yourself, from start to finish. You write the story, build the town as a film set in Roblox Studio, put your own avatar in it as the actor, move it, film it, and edit it into a 30–60 second video.

Here Roblox Studio isn't a game maker. It's your film studio. And it doesn't have to be scary: funny and weird rules work just as well.

What's attached

  • In This Town: Handbook (PDF): everything you need, lesson by lesson.
  • Your Story Doc (Google Doc, your own copy): rule card, story, shot list and working diary all live here.
  • InThisTown_Tutorial.rbxl: the practice place: a prop shop and eight stations (the set, movement, the camera, speech bubbles, rule effects, sound, your AI helper, a thumbnail booth). Open it in Roblox Studio, press Play and follow the arrows. Press F to watch a finished example film.

How every lesson works

  1. Read the yellow "Read first" box for that lesson in the handbook: 5 minutes.
  2. Watch the video for that lesson, if there is one.
  3. Do the work: most of the lesson.
  4. Diary: the last 5 minutes. Three lines in your Story Doc: what I did, what went wrong or was hard, what I'll do next.

The five lessons

Lesson Topic By the end you have
1 The story Rule card, story in 6 sentences and shot list in your Story Doc
2 The set Your town built in Studio, saved as name_inthistown_v2.rbxl
3 Actors and movement Your actors on the set, moving
4 Camera, talk, filming All your shots recorded and named shot_01, shot_02…
5 Editing and handing in Your finished video with captions, a title, a thumbnail and a caption, uploaded
Bonus Premiere We watch every film together, give feedback and hand out awards

No Studio until your story is written. No filming until your shot list is finished.

You'll probably need to work on it outside lessons too. That's fine, it's why it runs for a whole month.

Choose your level

You can always pick a higher level than the one for your grade. If your level feels like too much, talk to me and we pick the one that fits.

Hand in

  1. Video: 30–60 seconds, .mp4, the rule on screen in the first 2 seconds, captions throughout
  2. Studio file: your latest name_inthistown_vX.rbxl
  3. Title, thumbnail and a one-line caption
  4. Story Doc: rule card, story, shot list, a diary entry for every lesson with every AI use noted (Pro: plus your 150 words)

Edit in Canva, iMovie or DaVinci Resolve. The handbook shows all three.

Challenge: adapt one of Örkény István's one-minute stories (egyperces novellák) into your "In this town" video. The handbook lists the ones that work best.

Three rules that aren't up for discussion

No real people from school. No classmate as the victim or the target of the joke. No blood or gore.

The AI rule: AI helps, you direct

Use AI as a smart classmate. Gemini, ChatGPT and the Roblox Assistant can give you ideas for rules and twists, explain tools and settings, write and fix scripts for effects, cameras and animations, and give feedback on your shot list. The handbook has a whole chapter on asking it well and checking its code before you paste it.

Every AI use goes in your diary in one line: what you asked, and what you changed or learned.

Not allowed: AI writing your story for you, AI-generated video, and "one prompt" films where an AI builds the whole set and scene.

Questions?

The handbook has a Questions page at the back (Reference I) with the answers to the things everyone asks: accounts, two actors, missing sound, AI, music, pairs. The web version has a search box: type a word like "sound" or "animation" and it finds the answer. Still stuck? Ask a classmate, then me.

Grade 6 may work in pairs: one shared Story Doc, but each of you writes your own diary lines. English is preferred for the video, Hungarian is fine too.

Safety and files

  • Don't publish your place on Roblox. Save it as a file, and the video only goes to Classroom.
  • Raise the version number at the end of every lesson (v1, v2, v3…). If something breaks, you can go back.
  • Always log out of Roblox at the end of the lesson. These are shared computers.
  • Sound and music: from your editor's own library, or your own sounds. No famous songs. (Mac screen recording doesn't record the game's sound, so all sound goes in during editing.)
